About

I am a Data Scientist at VINCI (France) and a researcher affiliated with the Laboratoire Images, Signaux et Systèmes Intelligents (LISSI) at Universite Paris-Est Creteil Val-de-Marne (UPEC). In 2024 I obtained my PhD, titled "Conception de methodes d'optimisation basees sur la decomposition fractale. Application a la vision par ordinateur", under the supervision of Nadiya Shvai and Amir Nakib.

My academic journey began at the Universitat Autonoma de Barcelona (UAB), where I earned a Master's degree in Computer Vision and was a researcher at the Computer Vision Centre (CVC). Early work focused on eye-tracking systems using natural-light webcams, achieving a 34% reduction in gaze-estimation error over baseline methods.

My research now centres on the Fractal Decomposition Algorithm (FDA), a deterministic metaheuristic that recursively subdivides search spaces using geometric hyperspheres, and its applications to dynamic optimisation, neural-architecture search, model compression, and real-world computer-vision tasks.

Research Interests

Metaheuristic Optimisation Fractal Decomposition Algorithm Deep Learning Neural Architecture Search Model Compression Computer Vision Semantic Segmentation Image Anonymisation Gaze Estimation Autonomous Driving Intelligent Transport Systems Dynamic Optimisation Data Distillation Vision Transformers Evolutionary Computation
🌀

Fractal Decomposition Algorithm

Design and analysis of FDA, a deterministic metaheuristic that builds a search tree by recursively partitioning continuous solution spaces via geometric hyperspheres, applicable to large-scale and dynamic optimisation.

🧠

Deep Learning Optimisation

Neural-architecture search, hyperparameter optimisation, and model compression (pruning, quantisation) driven by evolutionary and fractal-based algorithms to deploy efficient models under resource constraints.

👁️

Computer Vision Applications

Semantic segmentation for tunnel roads, license plate recognition, urban traffic management, adaptive image anonymisation, and lightweight transformer architectures for edge deployment.

🛡️

Privacy and Anonymisation

Adaptive image anonymisation methods that balance privacy preservation with utility for downstream classification tasks, using black-box and evolutionary optimisation strategies.

🚗

Intelligent Transport Systems

Real-time CNN-based traffic management, automatic toll collection, camera alignment optimisation, and road infrastructure analysis using state-of-the-art deep learning models.

🔭

Gaze Estimation and HCI

Feature-based gaze estimation using natural-light webcams for multi-display systems, enabling low-cost human-computer interaction without specialised hardware.
